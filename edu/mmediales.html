Materias Mediales

María Torres + Arjan Guerrero

Materias Mediales offers an artistic research experience where we explore a continuity between creative and research practices in the face of emergent forms of artificialization.

The program usually comprises:

  • A module of theoretical foundations.
  • Two laboratories focused on developing a creative research project, combining exercises with tutorials and group discussions.
  • A series of sessions with invited practitioners, each introducing us to their own distinctive medial worlds.
