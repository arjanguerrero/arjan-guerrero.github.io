Arjan Guerrero

Arjan Guerrero is a London-based artist, creative technologist, and researcher currently working with computational simulations and agentic systems. His practice navigates emerging forms of artificialization and the worlds they bring about.

His work has been awarded the Lumen Prize and an Honorary Mention at Transitio_MX, supported by programs such as Arte Ciencia y Tecnologías and PAPIAM (MX), and presented individually in spaces like Somers Gallery (London) and Casa Maauad (Mexico City) as well as collectively across Mexico and Europe.

Holder of an MFA in Computational Arts from Goldsmiths, University of London, he is a Visiting Lecturer at the Winchester School of Art. Since 2021, he co-directs Materias Mediales, an educational programme on artistic research that brings together practising creatives and researchers in collaboration with varying institutions.
